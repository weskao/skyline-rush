# Player debuff HUD artwork

`freeze@3x.png` and `emp@3x.png` are the lossless production masters at 120 × 120 pixels. The HUD displays the transparent WebP exports from `public/feature-assets/player-debuff/` at 40 × 40 CSS pixels. The exports retain the entire cutout with at least 8 transparent pixels around its visible bounds.

The generated Freeze artwork is a six-armed faceted ice crystal with sapphire shadows and cyan highlights. The EMP artwork is a beveled lightning bolt with cobalt sides, a violet face and cold-white highlights. Both use a head-on silhouette and a transparent background for readability during a race.
