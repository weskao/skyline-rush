# Garage drift-trail artwork

Five ImageGen illustrations for the Garage Effects collection. The PNGs here are lossless 288×288 production masters. The cards load their WebP exports from `public/feature-assets/garage-vfx-skins/raster/` and display them at 96×96 CSS pixels. The five files replace the provisional thumbnails at the existing stable skin IDs.

The source was generated on transparent square canvases. Each full source was scaled down and centered by its visible alpha bounds without cropping the silhouette. The WebPs retain alpha. All five exports have at least 22 px clearance on every edge at alpha >16.

## Generation prompts

Shared composition

> Create one square transparent-alpha 2.5D / painterly 3D arcade-racing inventory collectible icon for a modern racing game Garage drift trail card, read at 96 px. Identical series composition: two closely spaced parallel tyre drift ribbons on an invisible ground plane, low bent diagonal sweeping from lower-left to upper-right in gentle complementary arcs, seen from high three-quarter overhead. Prominent clean silhouette, two separate track centers with a dark clear gap, broad shapes and layered sculptural depth, soft specular lighting, precise taper, controlled bright cores. Center the pair and occupy about 84% of the square with generous safe transparent padding, glow contained. Sole subject is the pair of tyre trails and its material particles. Absolutely transparent beyond the isolated effect; no checkerboard, no ground surface, no road piece, no vehicle, no base, no panel, no badge, no typographic marks, no UI, no text, no border or background.
