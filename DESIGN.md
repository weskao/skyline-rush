# Skyline Rush visual direction

Experience mode: the playable world fills the viewport. A coastal expressway at dusk, warm peach sky, dark blue water and a distant city. White electric sports car, red rear light bar and blue-white downward thrusters echo the supplied image. A narrow white editorial wordmark, coral accent and restrained telemetry frame the scene. The intro sits to the left while the car and road remain visible; racing hides the intro. Responsive HUD and large touch controls preserve the driving view.

No downloaded car models, Tesla logos, stock imagery or online asset dependencies. The car and road are real WebGL geometry. Fuel and altitude remain visible so flight's limits are understandable.
