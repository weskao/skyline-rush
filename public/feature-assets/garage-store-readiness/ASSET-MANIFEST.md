# Store Readiness — Non-transactional vector artwork

Two original transparent 96×96 SVG icons are committed independently of the future economy/checkout. They are **not yet wired into Store Vue UI, and do not enable or promise purchases**.

- `coming-soon.svg`: locked/unavailable storefront entry icon
- `collection.svg`: non-purchasable cosmetic collection category icon

Both are real transparent-background vector images (not checkerboard screenshots), with no monetary value, BUY controls, provider marks, localized text or embedded font. Future Vue should display honest editable `Coming soon` / `即將推出` text and an accessible disabled/no-payment state even when images fail. Live payments, balance and entitlement remain unimplemented and require separate approval. Original GPT-authored artwork.
